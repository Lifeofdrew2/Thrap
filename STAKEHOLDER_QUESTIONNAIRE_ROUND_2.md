# Employee Wellbeing Navigation Agent
## Second-round organisation questionnaire

Send this after the first-round answers establish a named human owner, out-of-hours cover, backup role, acknowledgement SLA, and failed-handoff procedure. It covers the information needed to configure the service and its approved knowledge base.

## Human service and routing

- Which human roles own routine wellbeing navigation, manager requests, safeguarding concerns, and privacy complaints?
- What channels and operating hours reach each role?
- What handoff SLA applies to each route?
- What is the approved backup path for each route?

## Approved services and content

- Which EAP providers and counsellor panel are authorised?
- What booking workflow, eligibility rules, languages, accessibility options, and operating hours are approved?
- Which HMO plan documents and coverage dates are authoritative?
- Which self-help materials are approved for indexing?
- Who owns review and expiry of each Markdown knowledge source?

## Privacy and retention

- What consent wording, privacy notice, DPO contact, and withdrawal process are approved?
- What are the retention periods for consent, booking, session state, escalation audit, metrics, and knowledge provenance?
- What controller/processor roles and access groups apply to employees, managers, HR, EAP staff, and support operators?
- What is the process for data-subject access, correction, deletion, and complaints?

## Evaluation and launch

- What reviewed labels and release thresholds define acceptable precision and recall per safety band?
- Which Nigerian English and Pidgin speakers will contribute reviewed red-team cases?
- What is the approved hard turn limit and handoff SLA for each intent?
- What operational metrics will be reviewed without tracking whether an employee received care?
- Who has final launch authority?

## Attachments requested

- approved escalation policy;
- named human roles, backup roles, hours, channels, and SLAs;
- current EAP overview and counsellor panel;
- confidentiality policy;
- HMO mental-health coverage documents;
- approved self-help library index;
- privacy notice and consent text;
- DPO/legal decision on offshore LLM processing;
- clinically reviewed crisis, refusal, and immediate-danger copy;
- handoff failure and notification acknowledgement procedure.

Do not send employee conversation samples or identifiable health information. Red-team examples should be newly authored, minimised, and reviewed through the organisation's approved process.
