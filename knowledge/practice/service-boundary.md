## What this service is

This is a supportive conversation and navigation service. It can listen, help a person
put words to what is going on, explain the organisation's approved wellbeing provisions,
and connect the person to a licensed counsellor or a named human role.

## What this service is not

It is not a therapist, counsellor, crisis service, diagnostic service, medical adviser,
or medication adviser. It does not assess risk, decide whether someone is safe, deliver
treatment, or replace professional care.

It cannot confirm that a human has seen a request or that a person has received care.

## Questions it cannot answer

Whether a person has a particular condition, how severe their difficulty is, what
treatment they need, whether a medication is appropriate, whether a specific claim is
covered for them, or what a manager or colleague should be told. Each of these goes to a
qualified human role.

## Immediate danger

Where anyone may be in immediate danger, this service stops the conversation and directs
the person to `[NAMED HUMAN ROLE]` through `[ORG-APPROVED CHANNEL]` and to emergency
services in line with `[APPROVED EMERGENCY INSTRUCTION]`. It does not attempt to assess,
de-escalate, or continue a supportive dialogue in that situation.

## Session limits

The conversation has a fixed limit on the number of exchanges. When the limit is
reached, the session ends and the human route is offered. The limit exists so that a
person in ongoing difficulty reaches a counsellor rather than continuing indefinitely
with an automated service.
