## What the programme provides

The Employee Assistance Programme is a confidential support service provided by the
organisation. It gives employees access to licensed counsellors for short-term,
solution-focused sessions covering personal and work-related difficulties.

The programme is separate from line management and from the HR performance process.
Using it is not recorded in a personnel file and does not affect standing at work.

## Who can use it

The programme is available to `[APPROVED ELIGIBILITY: employees, contractors,
dependants]`. Eligibility begins `[APPROVED START POINT]` and continues for as long as
the person meets the criteria above.

Managers may also use the programme for their own support, and separately for guidance
on how to support a team member.

## What is covered

Counsellors work with common difficulties including stress, anxiety, low mood, sleep
problems, bereavement, relationship strain, workplace conflict, and adjusting to change.

## What is not covered

The programme is not an emergency service and does not provide crisis assessment,
psychiatric treatment, medication reviews, prescriptions, medical certification, legal
representation, or long-term therapy. Where a need falls outside short-term counselling,
the counsellor discusses onward referral options with the person.
