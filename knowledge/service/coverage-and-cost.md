## Cost to the employee

Sessions provided under the Employee Assistance Programme are paid for by the
organisation. There is no charge to the employee for the sessions included in the
approved entitlement.

## Session entitlement

The approved entitlement is `[APPROVED NUMBER]` sessions per `[APPROVED PERIOD]`.
Where more support is helpful than the entitlement covers, the counsellor discusses
onward options, which may include the health plan or an external service.

## Health plan cover for mental health

Cover for mental health treatment beyond the programme depends on the plan the person
is enrolled in. The authoritative source is `[HMO PLAN DOCUMENT AND VERSION]`, which
sets out covered services, any limits, and how to make a claim.

This navigation service does not decide individual claims and cannot confirm whether a
specific treatment is covered for a specific person. Those questions go to
`[NAMED HUMAN ROLE]` through `[APPROVED CHANNEL]`.

## Time off to attend

Arrangements for attending during working hours follow `[APPROVED TIME-OFF POLICY]`.
A person does not have to give a reason beyond what that policy requires.

## Cover for family members

Whether dependants can use the programme is set by `[APPROVED ELIGIBILITY RULE]`.
